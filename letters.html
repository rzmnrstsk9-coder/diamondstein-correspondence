The Austrian Christian Ruse

“So I said I was an Austrian Christian — and they took me.”

Summary

Sam writes from Montreal to "Dear Darling Dora" on 11 June 1901. He's disappointed not to have heard from her that day, then turns to describing his hunt for work as a new immigrant: told that employers wouldn't take on Englishmen, he claimed to be an "Austrian Christian" and was hired on the spot, feeling uneasy watching that Jews were being turned away. Reading the contract more closely, he realized it required two months of unpaid labor to cover the cost of passage and food — but other people talked him out of taking the job, and he went back to the office that same day to reclaim what had been taken from him. Dora was only about 12 years old at this date, which makes "Darling" an unusually warm way to address her.
